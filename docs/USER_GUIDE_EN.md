# User Guide

## 1. About the service

This web application helps a school manage on-campus and take-home laptop rental requests, approvals, returns, and related records. Available screens and actions may differ depending on whether the account belongs to a student, staff member, helper, or administrator.

## 2. Signing in

1. Open the service URL.
2. Select the sign-in button.
3. Authenticate with a Google account permitted by the school.
4. On first sign-in, enter the requested profile information, such as name, student ID, grade, and class.

Always sign out after using the service on a shared computer.

## 3. Requesting a laptop

### Class rental

1. Select the class-rental menu.
2. Choose the year, month, week, weekdays, and periods.
3. Review the weekly timetable and submit the combined request.

### Take-home rental

1. Select the take-home rental menu.
2. Enter the requested period, reason, and other required information.
3. Review the school's equipment removal rules and submit the request.

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

## 4. Checking request status

Open the records menu to view your requests. Typical statuses include pending, approved, rejected, cancelled, and returned. You may cancel your own pending request. Approval, rejection, return results, and the assigned notebook number also appear in Notifications.

## 5. Staff and administrator functions

Authorized staff may review, approve, or reject requests for their assigned class. Approval automatically assigns an available notebook. After physically checking the device, staff record the return from the Records screen. Administrative access must only be used for authorized school duties and must never be shared.

## 6. Profile settings and sign-out

Use the settings screen to update profile fields that the school permits you to change. When finished, select sign out from the top navigation.

## 7. Troubleshooting

- If sign-in fails, confirm that you are using an account permitted by the school.
- If information is missing, refresh the page and sign in again.
- If your role is incorrect, ask the school operator to review your account permissions.
- If you suspect a privacy or security issue, contact the operator directly. Do not post screenshots containing personal information in a public forum.

## 8. User responsibilities

- Do not use another person's account.
- Do not share student information or rental records outside the school.
- Use equipment only for the approved purpose and period.
- Report loss, damage, or a security incident to school staff immediately.
