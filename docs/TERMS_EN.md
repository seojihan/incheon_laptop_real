These Terms govern access to and use of the school laptop rental management service (the “Service”) and describe the basic responsibilities of its users and operator.

Only students, school personnel, and other users authorized by the operating school may use the Service. Users must use their own accounts, protect their authentication information, and must not lend or share accounts.

## 4. Service functions

The Service may provide laptop rental requests, status tracking, approvals, rejections, returns, and related record management. Available functions and rental requirements may change according to school policy.

## 6. Care of borrowed equipment

Users must follow the school's checkout and return procedures and take reasonable care of borrowed equipment. Loss, damage, theft, or a security incident must be reported immediately. Financial responsibility and other consequences are determined by applicable law and school policy.

Users must keep submitted information accurate. If system records conflict with the physical condition or custody of equipment, the school may rely on signed checkout or return records and its authorized staff's verification.
